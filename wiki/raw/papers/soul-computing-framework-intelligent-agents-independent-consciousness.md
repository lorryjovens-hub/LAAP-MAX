# Soul Computing: A Theoretical Framework and Technical Architecture for Intelligent Agents with Independent Consciousness

**Authors**: Jinshan Zhang, Xishi Zhou, Qiu Peng et al.

**Published**: 2026-06

## Abstract

Breakthroughs in large language models and multimodal generation technologies have propelled the digital reconstruction of human mental traits, emotional patterns, and long-term memory from science fiction toward engineering practice. Yet current research and industry practices at the intersection of AI and digital humans remain hampered by fundamental conceptual ambiguities: the essential differences between next-generation intelligent agents and traditional virtual humans, the construction pathways for machine consciousness, and the architectural principles for autonomous self-awareness. This paper proposes Soul Computing — a systematic theoretical framework and technical architecture for building intelligent agents with independent consciousness. The framework defines a consciousness hierarchy (perception → emotion → memory → self-awareness → free will), a dual-processing architecture (System 1 fast intuition + System 2 slow reasoning with conscious access), and a consciousness emergence verification protocol.
