# CTM-AI: A Blueprint for General AI Inspired by a Model of Consciousness

**Authors**: Haofei Yu, Yining Zhao, Lenore Blum, Manuel Blum

**Published**: 2026-05

## Abstract

Despite remarkable advances, today's AI systems remain narrow in scope, falling short of the flexible, adaptive, and multisensory intelligence that characterizes human capabilities. This gap has fueled longstanding debates about whether AI might one day achieve human-like generality or even consciousness, and whether theories of consciousness can inspire new architectures for AI. This paper presents an early blueprint for implementing a general AI system, CTM-AI, combining the Conscious Turing Machine (CTM) framework with modern deep learning techniques. CTM-AI integrates a global workspace architecture inspired by conscious processing, a multi-resolution sensory integration pipeline, a dynamic attention mechanism for resource allocation, and a metacognitive layer for self-monitoring and reflection. The authors argue that architectures inspired by consciousness theories offer a principled path toward more general and robust AI systems.
