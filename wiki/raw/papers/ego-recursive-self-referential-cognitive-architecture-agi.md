# EGO: a Recursive and Self-Referential Cognitive Architecture for AGI

**Authors**: Alberto Mangiante, Paolo Totaro, Domenico Ninno

**Published**: 2025-07

## Abstract

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is interpreted as an emergent property of autonomous and self-organizing systems, grounded in the principles of autopoiesis and embodied cognition, overcoming the structural limitations of current Large Language Models (LLMs). We introduce EGO (Environment Generative Operator), a software architecture based on the formal E-language, capable of self-referentiality and of maintaining its internal organization, thereby realizing Maturana's autopoiesis and providing a computational foundation for consciousness. EGO is built on three layers: a sensorimotor interface, an autopoietic core that maintains self-organization, and a generative operator that produces novel representations. The architecture is demonstrated on a robot navigation task where EGO develops emergent goal-directed behavior without explicit reward functions.
