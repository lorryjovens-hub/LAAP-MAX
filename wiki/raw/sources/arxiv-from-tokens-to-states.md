## Summary

Argues LLMs are a special case of world models. True AGI requires abandoning autoregressive token prediction for latent-state world models (echoing LeCun 2022). Proposes continuous-state space models as the path beyond tokens. Directly supports LAAP's zero-LLM pipeline philosophy.
