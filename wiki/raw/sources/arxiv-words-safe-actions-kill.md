# When Words Are Safe But Actions Kill: Probing Physical Danger Beyond Text Safety in Hidden-State Risk Space

**Authors**: Weimeng Wang, Ziqiang Wang, Zihang Zhan
**Published**: 2026-07-16
**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CR

## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) increasingly serve as high-level planners for embodied agents, where linguistically benign instructions can become unsafe once grounded in the physical world. We study whether this physically grounded danger is the same safety problem as ordinary text-level content danger.

## LAAP Relevance

This paper addresses a critical gap in AI safety — the disconnect between text-level safety and physical-world safety for embodied agents. As LAAP moves toward embodied AI integration, understanding this hidden-state risk space is essential.

## Key Insights

1. Benign text instructions can cause physical harm when grounded
2. Proposes hidden-state risk space for detecting physical danger
3. Text-level safety ≠ physical-world safety
