# DemoPSD: Disagreement-Modulated Policy Self-Distillation

**Authors:** Yunhe Li, Hao Shi, Wenhao Liu, Mengzhe Ruan, Hanxu Hou, Zhongxiang Dai, Shuang Qiu, Linqi Song

**Published:** 2026-07-02 | **Category:** cs.LG, cs.AI

## Abstract

On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) has emerged as a practical method for training large language models (LLMs) to reason. However, recent studies have found that the teacher's dense token-level supervision might not be optimal. DemoPSD proposes disagreement-modulated distillation to address this limitation.

## Relevance to LAAP

Relevant to [[aris-cognitive-loop]]'s learning and self-improvement mechanisms. The disagreement-modulated approach could inform our [[psi-self-training]] engine's distillation strategies.

## Key Insights

1. Token-level supervision in self-distillation can be suboptimal
2. Disagreement modulation improves distillation quality
3. Efficiency gains in LLM reasoning training
