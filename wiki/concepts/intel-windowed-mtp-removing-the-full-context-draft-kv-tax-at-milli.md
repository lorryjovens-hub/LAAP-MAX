# Windowed-MTP: Removing the Full-Context Draft-KV Tax at Million-Token Context

**来源**: arxiv | **综合评分**: 7.5/10
**四维**: 质量=8 | 安全=5 | 创新=8 | 效率=9

## 摘要

Speculative decoding with built-in MTP draft head: at million-token context, draft-KV recomputation becomes dominant. Windowed-MTP removes this tax by limiting draft to a sliding window, achieving significant speedup at long context.
